HOW IT WORKS

You run mission control for freight across the solar system. Every world sells some goods cheaply and buys others at a premium — click a planet to see its lists, or open markets for every price at once, price history, the best routes and contract offers. Cargo is sold automatically on arrival wherever that world buys it.

Planning: click a docked ship. Pick a destination (or click a planet and use “send here”), add cargo with − / + (shift-click for max). The chart shows Δv (fuel) for each departure date (x) and flight time (y): bright green is cheap, dark means the ship can't carry enough fuel with that load. Click the chart to pick, then schedule.

Prices move: your trades push prices (big orders pay more per tonne), local industry restocks over a few months, economies drift, and news can spike or crash a market. Food spoils and helium-3 boils off on long flights.

Money: you start with a $4M loan due in two years. Interest is charged monthly; borrow or repay from the finance screen (click your balance). Miss a due date and you pay a late fee; miss it again and the lender seizes ships.

Bases: build an orbital depot (no lift fee), a trading post (20% off goods) or a warehouse (store cargo) at a site from the planet panel. They cost upkeep every month.

Rivals: two other companies trade by the same rules and move the same markets. Compare net worth on the finance screen.

Controls: wheel zoom · drag pan · click a body or ship to follow · 1 inner system · 0 outer system · space pause · +/- speed · Esc close.
On a phone: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap planets & ships. The tabs at the bottom switch between your fleet, the selected planet, the selected ship and the log — tap the open tab again to hide it and see the whole map.
