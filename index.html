HOW IT WORKS

You run mission control for freight across the solar system. Every world sells some goods cheaply and buys others at a premium — click a planet to see its lists. Buy where it's cheap, fly, and cargo is sold automatically on arrival wherever that world buys it. Anything a world doesn't buy stays aboard for the next leg.

Planning: click a docked ship. Pick a destination (or click a planet and use “send here”), add cargo with − / + (shift-click for max). The chart shows Δv (fuel) for each departure date (x) and flight time (y): bright green is cheap, dark means the ship can't carry enough fuel with that load. Click the chart to pick, then schedule.

Ships: Haulers carry 40 t on a chemical-nuclear drive — fine for the inner system. Clippers carry 20 t on a fusion drive and are what you need for Mercury and the outer planets.

Costs: cargo, propellant (local price — very cheap at the Moon and the gas-giant stations), and lift per tonne launched. Earth, Mars, Venus and the giants allow aerobraking, so arriving there is cheap.

Controls: wheel zoom · drag pan · click a body or ship to follow · 1 inner system · 0 outer system · space pause · +/- speed · Esc close.
On a phone: drag to pan, pinch to zoom, tap planets & ships. The tabs at the bottom switch between your fleet, the selected planet, the selected ship and the log — tap the open tab again to hide it and see the whole map. Tap or drag on the transfer chart to preview a window; “max” fills the hold.
